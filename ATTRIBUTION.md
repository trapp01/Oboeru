## Clips: `site/audio/`

Every clip is synthesized speech, made by `scripts/generate-audio.mjs` with
[VOICEVOX](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/), voice 四国めたん. The clips are not
covered by the MIT licence or by CC BY. They may be used under:

- the [VOICEVOX terms of use](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/term/), which ask
  that anyone the audio is passed on to follows the voice's terms too, and
- the [voice's terms of use](https://zunko.jp/con_ongen_kiyaku.html), which
  allow commercial and non-commercial use with a credit, and forbid some uses
  (among them political or religious activity, and deliberately false or
  misleading content).

Credit, in the form the voice asks for, wherever the audio is used:

    VOICEVOX:四国めたん

The app shows this line under the lesson list. Keep it there if you deploy the
clips, or replace the clips and the line together if you generate your own
with another voice.

The mnemonic format follows the keyword method of Atkinson and Raugh (1975).
